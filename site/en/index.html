A LITTLE PROMISE, MADE CLEAR

Every step deserves
an answer.

Keep the whole story, from proposal to receipt.
The app currently uses a Chinese interface; all screenshots show synthetic sample data.

01

Agree on the details

Set the goal, conditions, deadline, and reward in three steps. The agreement begins when the other person accepts.

02

Notice the effort

Record progress and submit words or photos for review. Changes to agreed terms need mutual confirmation, too.

03

Follow through

Completion and reward delivery are separate. A reward is settled when its recipient confirms receipt. Ending a relationship does not erase an earned reward.

Inside
A native Android client and a Python standard-library backend, with source code and setup instructions in the repository.
Boundaries
No hosted production backend, background push notifications, payments, or AI arbitration. Parent-recorded actions are explicitly attributed.
