A LITTLE PROMISE, MADE CLEAR

Every step deserves
an answer.

Keep the whole story, from proposal to fulfillment.
The app currently uses a Chinese interface; all screenshots show synthetic sample data.

01

Agree on the details

Set the goal, conditions, deadline, and reward in three steps. The agreement begins when the other person accepts.

02

Notice the effort

Creation, acceptance, counts, notes, and photos share one progress history. Submit completed work for review; changes to agreed terms need mutual consent.

03

Follow through

Task completion and reward fulfillment are separate. The giver marks a reward fulfilled to close it; no second confirmation from the recipient is required. Ending a relationship does not erase an earned reward.
